Control compatible dimmable and tunable-white lights directly through local Bluetooth, with no phone or gateway needed for everyday operation. Choose whole luminaires or separate channel devices and use them in your lighting Flows.

This is an independent community integration, not affiliated with Casambi Technologies Oy or Athom. An existing authorized Evolution network and a one-time setup or offline credential export are required. Compatibility varies by fixture and firmware; consult the project documentation before installation.
